Growth Partners — 2026 Rebuild

Digital done differently — the Awwwards-style marketing site.

The canonical Growth Partners rebuild (repo growth-partners-2026): plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, GSAP + a Three.js particle hero. Mal delivered the LOCAL concept; the BUILD phase self-hosted all dependencies, moved onto preserved clean URLs, and — after a design-system v2 expansion (~15 components) — migrated the 70 Keep/Improve pages from search data (33 blogs with reused imagery, NZ + AU location pages, case studies, services, DigitalArchitect®, pricing, plus the full /en-au/ Australian mirror). 76 pages live on growth-partners.pages.dev, full SEO, nav pruned to value + NZ/AU switcher. Supersedes the Astro effort. What's left: a UI/UX motion pass, and the CF-account items (staging domain, contact email, analytics).
