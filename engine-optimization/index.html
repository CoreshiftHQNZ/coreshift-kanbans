Engine Optimization

The internal tool Growth Partners' SEO specialists run their clients on — audit, plan, do, measure, report, re-plan.

A web app for managing SEO/AEO/GEO clients on a monthly cycle. Everything that can run unattended does: crawls, Search Console and GA4 pulls, AI-citation probes, delta analysis, plan drafting, report drafting. A human signs off before anything touches a client site or reaches a client. The thing that makes it different from every tool we surveyed: it records what we predicted a change would do, then checks — against a control — whether it happened.

Current phase M1 · Data spine
Next milestone One client's GSC + GA4 data visible in the app Q3 2026

The Three Goals

🎯
Specialists, not engineers
A team of SEO specialists runs the whole monthly cycle in a web app. Nobody opens a terminal.
🔁
Close the assumption loop
Record what we expect a change to do, then verify it against an untouched control set next month. No prior art does this.
📈
Built for 10, designed for 100
Ten clients today. Multi-client scheduling, per-client history and zero-touch Google access from day one.

Phase 2 Plan and do
M4 · after measurement is trusted

Turn six separate priority lists into one ranked month of work that fits the retainer. The aggregator is the piece no surveyed repo has.

  • Cross-source finding merge + dedupe (a missing H1 appears in three audits)
  • Single priority function, effort estimates, owners, capacity fit
  • Work items carry a required rationale — it becomes the report's 'why'
  • Human approval gate before anything touches a client site

Planned
Phase 3 Close the loop
M5–M6 · the differentiator

Record the prediction, build the control, check it next cycle, and say so in the report. This is the part clients pay a retainer for and the part nothing else does.

  • Readback windows fixed before a change, not chosen after
  • Matched control-set constructor — demanded by every repo, built by none
  • Delta-vs-control, with algorithm updates auto-flagged as confounders
  • Monthly report: what we did, why, what we expected, what happened
