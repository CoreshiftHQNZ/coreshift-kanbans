What the discovery turned up

Findings that changed the plan, not just filled it in.

The team bios were nearly lost. All 12 bios live on /team-blocks — a NOINDEX page absent from the sitemap, reachable only via popup anchors. A sitemap-driven harvest drops 1,341 words covering every person in the firm and nobody notices until the client asks. Recovered.
Those bios are invisible to Google today. Because they sit on a NOINDEX page, none of that content ranks. Moving it inline on /our-team makes it crawlable for the first time — the single biggest free SEO gain in the project.
33 of 35 URLs keep their exact path. Same domain, same slugs, www stays canonical. Only /home → / and /team-blocks → /our-team need 301s. This is about as low-risk as a migration gets — provided nobody "tidies" a slug.
The site has no analytics at all. No GA4, no GTM, no pixel — only Squarespace's built-in, which doesn't survive. Being provisioned fresh. Historical data must be exported before the account lapses or the firm's entire baseline is gone.
Four blog images look unlicensed. Filenames indicate a Getty asset and images pulled off news sites and Google Images. Quarantined rather than migrated — republishing them on a law firm's own site isn't a call to make silently. Default is to replace with licensed stock.
