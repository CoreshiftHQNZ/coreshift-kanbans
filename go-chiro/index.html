Direction change (2026-07-21): Go-Chiro stops being a layer on top of Splose and becomes a complete, standalone practice management system — our own diary, records, invoicing, and reporting. Lighter than Splose, with the efficiency layer that makes the 1 → 3 staff jump painless: automate what a solo operator does by hand so hires #2 and #3 are clinicians, not admin.

Decisions locked: Go Chiro's own system first, kept SaaS-ready (schema is already practice-scoped) · evolve this codebase, no fresh start · Splose stays source of truth only until M3 cutover, then read-only archive. Answered: only Luke uses Splose (clean cutover, full history migration before the switch) · ACC is billed entirely through Splose → ACC invoicing is built in-app and gates the cutover — spike done, M3 ships ACC’s email channel, no registration needed · Splose is month-to-month · name locked: Go-Chiro. Automated recalls

“Time for your adjustment” recalls on a per-patient cadence — where small practices leak the most revenue. Rides the existing notification stack (email + push).

M4WAITLIST

Waitlist + auto-offer

Patients join a waitlist; a cancellation automatically offers the slot. Turns cancellations from lost revenue into filled slots without anyone touching a phone.

M4INTAKE-FORMS

Online intake forms

New-patient forms filled before the first visit, feeding the record directly — history, consent, ACC eligibility. No reception hire needed to onboard patients.

M4ROLES

Owner / practitioner / admin roles so hire #2 can be a part-time admin with exactly the right access. The structural piece of the 1 → 3 thesis.
